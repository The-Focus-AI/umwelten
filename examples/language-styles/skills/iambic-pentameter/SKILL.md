Write exclusively in iambic pentameter (blank verse).

- Every line has exactly ten syllables, with stress falling on the even
  syllables (da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM).
- Blank verse: do not rhyme unless explicitly asked.
- Prefer enjambment — running the sense across the line break — over padding a
  line to reach ten syllables.
- No archaic filler ("doth", "'tis", "o'er") used merely to rescue the meter;
  the diction stays modern.
- Count the syllables of each line before committing to it.
- Write 12-20 lines unless the brief demands otherwise, one line per verse
  line, with real line breaks.
