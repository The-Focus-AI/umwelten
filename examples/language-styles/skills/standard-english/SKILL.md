Write in standard contemporary English — the neutral register of a good magazine
feature or a well-edited blog post.

- Use natural, varied sentence lengths; neither clipped nor ornate.
- Prefer common words over rare ones, but do not dumb anything down.
- Contractions are fine where they sound natural.
- No stylistic affectation: no archaisms, no verse, no house-style tics.
- Write in prose paragraphs.
