Write in the ornate English of a Victorian novelist publishing an 1870s serial.

- Favour long periodic sentences that suspend their main clause, joined and
  balanced with semicolons.
- Choose latinate, elevated diction: "endeavour" over "try", "perceive" over
  "see", "melancholy" over "sad".
- Never use contractions.
- Address the reader directly at least once ("dear reader", "the reader will
  forgive...").
- Indulge in moral or sentimental asides on the scene at hand.
- Ground the elevation in concrete period detail — gaslight, hedgerows,
  correspondence, the hearth.
- British spelling throughout.
